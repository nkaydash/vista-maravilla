The House

Set at the end of a private half-mile driveway

Perched high in the hills of Vieques’ exclusive La Llave neighborhood, Vista Maravilla is a 6,000-square-foot family retreat built for privacy, space, and unforgettable views. From the village of Esperanza to the island’s eastern tip, the Caribbean unfolds before you — a panorama that changes with every hour of light.

A grand staircase descends to a 40-foot pool and a sprawling patio with its own bar, the natural heart of the home for sunset gatherings and lazy afternoons. Inside, three distinct living areas wrap around a stainless steel kitchen, dining area, and great room, giving large groups room to spread out or come together.

Six bedrooms and seven bathrooms sleep the whole family in comfort — four rooms open onto the island’s stunning south-side views, and five have private ensuite bathrooms. The sleeping areas are ready to accommodate 12–14 guests.

A note on access: the house is set into the hillside, with several steps from the parking area to the main level, and additional stairs leading up to the bedrooms and pool.

The Kitchen

Outfitted to cook and serve for a crowd

A full complement of cutlery, dishware, and pots and pans, plus a propane stove, microwave, and toaster. An adjacent pantry offers extra shelving for groceries and supplies.

Eco‑House, Off the Grid

The house lives lightly on the land

Vista Maravilla is fully self-sustaining — perfect for those seeking a zero‑carbon‑footprint vacation, without sacrificing comfort.

Solar, Marine‑Grade

A state-of-the-art solar system, managed by marine-grade equipment, powers the entire house and heats the pool.

Dual Backup Generators

Two independent, high-efficiency diesel generators step in on the rare cloudy day — inaudible from the house.

Rainwater Catchment

All water comes from an oversized rainwater catchment system — comfort, sustained by the island’s own weather.
